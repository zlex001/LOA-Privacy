Privacy Policy for Log of Adventurers

Effective Date: June 6, 2026

Log of Adventurers is a text-driven online RPG developed by Zhang Xi.

Information We Collect

Log of Adventurers may collect the following information:

  • User ID
  • Device ID
  • Gameplay data, including character data, progress, inventory, map state, battle records, quests, and other game-related records
  • User-generated content, including player nicknames, chat messages, and other text submitted in the game
  • Purchase history related to in-app purchases
  • Product interaction data, such as app launches, gameplay actions, and in-game interactions

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, videos, microphone recordings, health data, financial account information, or sensitive personal information.

How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the game
  • Save character progress and gameplay records
  • Enable online gameplay features
  • Enable chat and other user-generated content features
  • Process and verify in-app purchases
  • Maintain game security and prevent abuse
  • Provide customer support when needed

User-Generated Content

Log of Adventurers includes online text interaction features. Users may create or submit content such as nicknames and chat messages.

Users are responsible for the content they submit. We may review, restrict, remove, or take action against inappropriate content or abusive behavior when necessary to maintain the game environment.

In-App Purchases

Log of Adventurers may offer in-app purchases. Payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store payment card numbers or other financial account details.

Tracking and Advertising

Log of Adventurers does not use collected data to track users across other apps or websites.

Log of Adventurers does not currently show third-party advertisements.

Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

We may share limited data only when necessary to operate the game service, comply with legal obligations, protect users or the service, or process platform services such as App Store in-app purchases.

Data Retention

We retain game-related data for as long as necessary to provide the game service, maintain account and character progress, resolve disputes, enforce rules, and comply with legal obligations.

Children

Log of Adventurers is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

User Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To request assistance, contact us by email.
