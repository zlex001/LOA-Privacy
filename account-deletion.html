Account and Data Deletion for Log of Adventurers

Log of Adventurers is developed by Paramedise.

Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Log of Adventurers account and related data, email us at:

paramedisedeveloper@gmail.com

Please include your account ID and device ID if available. If you do not know them, provide enough information for us to identify the account.

Data Deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete or anonymize account data, gameplay progress, character data, inventory records, game logs, and user-generated content where applicable.

Data That May Be Retained

Some records may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or payment verification purposes. This may include limited transaction, purchase, abuse-prevention, or service operation records.

Processing Time

We aim to process deletion requests within 30 days after receiving enough information to verify the account.
